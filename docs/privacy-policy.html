Privacy Policy — Internet TV Player

Last updated: 15 September 2026  ·  No data collected

Internet TV Player is an Android app that plays IPTV you already have access to. It is a player only: it does not provide, host, or sell any channels, and it has no accounts. This policy explains, honestly and in full, what the app does with information.

The short version

Nothing about you is collected, transmitted to us, or shared with anyone. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting, and no server of ours that the app talks to.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your playlists and Xtream Codes details (server URL, username, password, channel list) are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • Your settings (interface mode, cast mode, user agent, favourites) are also stored only on your device.
  • We never receive any of it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What leaves your device

  • Your IPTV provider. The app requests your playlist and streams directly from the server you configured. Your credentials go to that server and nowhere else.
  • Chromecast (only when you cast). If your stream is MPEG-TS, the app converts it to HLS on your phone and serves it to your TV over your local Wi-Fi. The stream is not sent anywhere else, and the relay stops when casting stops.
  • Google Play (Play build only). If you installed the app from Google Play, the app may ask Google Play whether a newer version exists, using the Play In-App Update API. That request is handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. The app sends no information of its own.
  • GitHub (direct-download build only). If you installed the APK from GitHub Releases, the app may request api.github.com/repos/Zcc09/iptv-player/releases/latest to see whether a newer version exists. That request contains no personal data, no identifier, and no information about your playlists or viewing. If you tap download, the new APK is fetched from GitHub.

Permissions and why they exist

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — fetch playlists and play streams.
  • ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE — find Chromecast devices on your local network.
  • WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE* — keep playback and the cast relay running while the app is in the background.
  • REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES — not present in the Google Play build. It exists only in the direct-download build so it can install its own update, and it is used for nothing else.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone — including children.

Content responsibility

Internet TV Player ships with no playlists, no credentials, and no content. You supply the playlist or Xtream account, and you are responsible for having the right to watch what you load.
